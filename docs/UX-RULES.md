# UX Rules — 🏭 HAKIMO — نظام إدارة الإنتاج

- Begin with user goal, fastest successful path, before/after context, permissions, and recovery.
- Preserve the business requirement while removing avoidable friction; validate uncertain rules.
- Keep one obvious primary action and subordinate secondary actions.
- Design loading, empty, error, partial, stale, offline/network, disabled, read-only, validation, success, and permission-denied states.
- Keep validation near its cause; use toasts only for transient confirmation.
- Choose page, drawer/sheet, modal, or popover according to workflow depth and screen size.
- Mobile reprioritizes content rather than shrinking desktop. Avoid horizontal scrolling except inherently wide data.
- Verify RTL/LTR navigation, arrows, breadcrumbs, drawers, inputs, pagination, tables, charts, and mixed-direction content.
- Accessibility is definition of done: semantics, headings, labels, names, keyboard flow, focus, contrast, and touch targets.
- Solve root causes before adding local overrides, duplicated state, or dependencies.

For this **operations/admin product**, favor trustworthy feedback, concise hierarchy, preserved context, and efficient completion of the primary task.
